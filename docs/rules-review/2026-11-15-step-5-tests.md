## Non-keep

### duplicate (1)

1. test/mailcore.test.ts:144 "the vendor call a network failure is vendor_down, never a throw" - duplicate.
   Compared with test/seams.test.ts:260 "seam: mail vendor transport a vendor call through the default transport comes back as vendor_down, not a throw". Both call sendViaVendor(cfg, mail) with a vendor that cannot be reached (mailcore: injected fetch throwing TypeError "connection reset"; seams: default transport to an unresolvable host, which throws a TypeError) and assert only r.kind === "vendor_down". seams adds the "no illegal invocation" check, so it is the stronger of the two. Action: delete mailcore.test.ts:144; already covered by seams.test.ts:260.
